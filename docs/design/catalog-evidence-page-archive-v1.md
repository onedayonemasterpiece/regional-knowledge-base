## Bibliographic distinctions retained in the current product

These domain distinctions remain applicable, without requiring a second catalog service or large new bibliographic graph:

- Distinguish a work/group of editions, a concrete book edition/serial issue/article, an uploaded file/source and a processing revision. Reprocessing is not a new edition. Two scans may represent one edition while retaining distinct source hashes. Similar titles or an ISBN alone do not authorize identity merging.
- Keep `publication`, `serial`, source/component links and exact source revisions. `work_group_id` is optional and explicitly established. Issue/volume/supplement labels are strings; unknown years/container details remain unknown. A standalone article is valid without a known journal.
- An article within an issue shares its source bytes; it has its own contributors/title/identifiers and exact region/span boundaries. A separate article PDF is another source copy unless an explicit mapping is accepted. Do not duplicate accepted search projections automatically.
- Preserve `semantic_unit_id` and article boundaries through chunks, footnotes and continuation expansion. A distant continuation needs an explicit link. Two articles on one page need region/span ranges; a page range alone is insufficient.
- Bibliographic fields retain title/subtitle/alternative titles, contributors, languages, publication date and precision, publisher/place, edition/volume/issue, identifiers, source annotation, separately labelled model summary, classifications/subjects/geographic and time coverage, cover descriptor and parent relations.
- Contributors retain role/order/provenance and optional person/organization identity: author, editor, translator, compiler, illustrator, photographer, corporate author and extensible roles. Do not make the first contributor principal author or the issue editor author of every article. `authors[]` can remain a compatibility projection.
- Identifiers keep scheme/raw/normalized/validation status/provenance, including ISBN-10/13, ISSN/eISSN, DOI and local IDs. Deterministic checksum validation does not authorize guessing a corrected number. Preserve invalid raw values; support old books without modern identifiers.
- A printed source annotation, model summary, user metadata and external registry enrichment have separate provenance. External enrichment is optional. Field corrections do not re-embed unchanged text.
- Use real cover/title-page assets with accurate labels. An issue cover shown for an article is explicitly `container_cover`; a title page is not falsely called a front cover and no generated cover substitutes for source evidence. Missing cover does not fail text import. Apply authoritative artifact class/access to its actual pixels.
- Resource type, publication form, audience/purpose, subject and coverage are independent versioned extensible vocabularies. Classification does not certify truth. New genres do not require a closed database enum or mandatory UDC/BBK generation.

Catalog listing/search keeps stable bounded pagination and separate publication/source/revision counts, text/index readiness and visual availability. List can include an explicit empty-query browse scenario; unfinished imports are distinguishable from accepted publications. Preserve compatible existing tools and actual MCP registration/client visibility rather than adding low-level methods by inertia. See [MCP](../mcp.md), [ingestion](../ingestion.md) and [article scope](../operations/article-search-scope.md).

## Exact quotation and geometry distinctions retained

Quotation is selected from immutable accepted source text, not regenerated by the model. A selector binds source/revision/region/span and Unicode code-point `[start,end)` offsets; its SHA is over exact UTF-8 bytes. Normalized search offsets need alignment back to source. Ordered multi-page fragments retain physical pages/printed labels, component identity and hashes. Editorial ellipsis/separators are marked separately; a footnote is linked, not silently joined. An image-only source may use accepted model transcription with uncertainty; its hash does not prove a flawless reading of the print.

Precise proof needs source-range-to-page geometry, native PDF quads where valid or model-supplied coordinates for the authorized scan. Keep renderer/dimensions/cropbox/rotation/transform and preview hash. Block bounds or copied bounds of split text are not exact character highlights. Columns, repeated phrases, rotated lines, ligatures/hyphens and remote continuation require their own mapping. If alignment is unavailable, report the actual accuracy or unavailable state; do not paint broad invented stripes.

Use bounded temporary renditions, actual MIME/ImageContent, per-item partial results, separate source/display/provider/delivery hashes and preview dependencies until callbacks terminate. No permanent Telegram highlight per question and no all-pages archive prerequisite. See [illustration/proof operations](../operations/illustration-pipeline.md) and [read continuation](../operations/archive-read-continuation.md).

## Current access and recovery replace the old gates

Accepted active book text/bibliography and accepted illustration renditions are public. Proof stripes/page/crop scans are available to owner/demo/expert/local_historian via existing `scan_proof_read`; full originals remain independently private. Verified categories materialize audited grants under [rights.md](../rights.md); current grants authorize rendering, with claimed-category denial, demo expiry/category removal and respected manual revocation. Classification must reflect actual geometry: public figures cannot contain surrounding restricted quote scans, and a client cannot relabel a page as an illustration. Owner policy does not fabricate legal-status evidence or require new per-figure approvals. Recheck source withdrawal/current policy, grants and audience at cache/callback/delivery; shared Slack remains `public_only`. See [rights](../rights.md).

Source archival uses exact native topic 2 originals and topic 4 illustration readback, stable operation IDs and verified hashes before byte GC. One uploader and existing provider pacing cover all relevant topics. Bounded cache/journal/checkpoint/backup retention and real Dataset version GC follow the current handoff; the retired document's quota calculations or preserved-pinned-content assumptions do not authorize old non-GEO migration or unlimited retention.

Historical source references and measurements belong to the immutable design linked above. They are not new legal determinations, current provider quotas or evidence that the new implementation passes acceptance.
